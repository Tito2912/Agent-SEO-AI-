## Overview

**Parties.** This agreement binds:

- **the Customer**: the legal or natural person acting in a business capacity who uses the Noyaru
  service and who, for the processing described in Annex II, is the **controller**;
- **E-Com Shop**, SASU, 60 rue François 1er, 75008 Paris, France, 934 934 308 R.C.S. Paris, publisher
  of the Noyaru service, which acts as **processor** for this processing.

In this agreement, “controller” means the Customer and “processor” means E-Com Shop.

**Scope.** This agreement governs the processing E-Com Shop carries out on the Customer's behalf. It
does not apply to the processing for which E-Com Shop is itself responsible (account, subscription,
billing, support, security), described in the privacy policy (noyaru.com/en/privacy). Requests about
that processing fall under that policy and are not passed on to the Customer.

**Basis.** As Article 28(6) GDPR allows, this agreement is based in part on the standard contractual
clauses of Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/915 of 4 June 2021. Part A reproduces their
official text, with the options stated below, except on the following two points. Because it departs
from them on these points, this agreement is not a full adoption of those clauses.

#### Clause 9 — Notification of personal data breach

In the event of a personal data breach, the processor shall cooperate with and assist the controller for the controller to comply with its obligations under Articles 33 and 34 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 or under Articles 34 and 35 of Regulation (EU) 2018/1725, where applicable, taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to the processor.

##### 9.1. Data breach concerning data processed by the controller

In the event of a personal data breach concerning data processed by the controller, the processor shall assist the controller:

(a) in notifying the personal data breach to the competent supervisory authority/ies, without undue delay after the controller has become aware of it, where relevant/(unless the personal data breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons);

(b) in obtaining the following information which, pursuant to Article 33(3) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, shall be stated in the controller’s notification, and must at least include:

(1) the nature of the personal data including where possible, the categories and approximate number of data subjects concerned and the categories and approximate number of personal data records concerned;

(2) the likely consequences of the personal data breach;

(3) the measures taken or proposed to be taken by the controller to address the personal data breach, including, where appropriate, measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects.

Where, and insofar as, it is not possible to provide all this information at the same time, the initial notification shall contain the information then available and further information shall, as it becomes available, subsequently be provided without undue delay.

(c) in complying, pursuant to Article 34 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, with the obligation to communicate without undue delay the personal data breach to the data subject, when the personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

##### 9.2. Data breach concerning data processed by the processor

In the event of a personal data breach concerning data processed by the processor, the processor shall notify the controller without undue delay after the processor having become aware of the breach. Such notification shall contain, at least:

(a) a description of the nature of the breach (including, where possible, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and data records concerned);

(b) the details of a contact point where more information concerning the personal data breach can be obtained;

(c) its likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed to be taken to address the breach, including to mitigate its possible adverse effects.

Where, and insofar as, it is not possible to provide all this information at the same time, the initial notification shall contain the information then available and further information shall, as it becomes available, subsequently be provided without undue delay.

The Parties shall set out in Annex III all other elements to be provided by the processor when assisting the controller in the compliance with the controller’s obligations under Articles 33 and 34 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

## Annex III — Technical and organisational measures

### Security measures

These are the measures actually applied on the date of this agreement. E-Com Shop may change them
without reducing the overall level of security.

- **Protection during transmission**: encrypted connections (HTTPS) enforced in the browser (HSTS); TLS
  1.2 or higher at the host.
- **Protection during storage**: account passwords kept only as a hash (PBKDF2); tokens, keys and
  application passwords entrusted by the Customer encrypted by the service; database and disks encrypted
  at rest by the host Render (according to its contract); backups and analysis files encrypted at rest by
  Amazon S3 (encryption applied by default by that service).
- **Identification and authorisation**: individual accounts; protected session cookies (HttpOnly,
  SameSite, Secure over HTTPS); access control by account and by project; protection of forms against
  request forgery (CSRF); rate limiting; anti-bot check on sign-up; operations screens reserved for the
  owner of the platform; two-factor authentication on E-Com Shop's GitHub, Render, AWS and Stripe
  accounts; Amazon S3 storage access key belonging to a dedicated user, without console access, and
  limited to the service's single bucket (list, read, write, delete).
- **Application protection**: content security policy (CSP) and a ban on embedding pages in an external
  frame (X-Frame-Options).
- **Event logging**: audit log of sensitive actions.
- **Handling of data breaches**: written procedure of 12 November 2026; register of all incidents,
  including those that are not notified; containment measures; notification of the Customer under
  supplementary clause B4.
- **Availability and restoration**: daily database backup, kept for 30 days. An automatic test restores
  the latest backup into a disposable database every month and checks its content; first successful test
  on 6 November 2026.
- **Testing and evaluation**: every code change goes through an automatic gate (tests, security analysis
  of the code, dependency audit). This agreement does not claim any certification or independent security
  audit.
- **Default configuration**: no change is merged into the Customer's repository without its validation.
  When the Customer deliberately enables automatic merging or publication, the changes concerned may be
  applied without prior human review.
- **Minimisation**: error traces sent to the tracking service contain no code variables, forms, cookies
  or address parameters; email addresses are masked. A name written in the text of an error remains
  possible.
- **Limited retention and erasure**: deleting a project or an account erases the corresponding data from
  the active database and its analysis files, on disk and in remote storage; backups disappear 30 days
  after they are created.
- **Staff access**: on the date of this agreement, only the director of E-Com Shop accesses the data
  processed for the Customer, and remains bound to confidentiality under this agreement. Before any other person
  has access, E-Com Shop has them sign a confidentiality undertaking.
- **Workstation**: the disk of the director's workstation is encrypted.
- **Physical security**: the data is hosted by Render and Amazon Web Services, whose data centres fall
  under their own measures.

**TO BE CONFIRMED by E-Com Shop before publication, only measures actually applied being listed**:
actual deletion of backups at 30 days.

## Part B — Supplementary clauses

### B1. Acceptance and versions

**Acceptance.** This agreement is accepted expressly, online, by a person authorised to bind the Customer,
who ticks the box provided for that purpose in the service (“My account → Data processing agreement”).
That person represents that they hold this authority and enters the name of the organisation, its
registration number where one exists, their name and their position.

date and time, the version, the SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact text accepted and its language, the IP
address and the browser, for the whole term of the agreement and then for five years. This evidence

**Versions.** Each version is dated and numbered. E-Com Shop informs the Customer of a new version before
it takes effect. A version that changes the rights or obligations of the parties only applies after a new
express acceptance; until then, the version previously accepted remains applicable, unless the change is
required by applicable law. Updates to the Annexes follow Clause 2(a) and, for the list in Annex IV,
Clause 7.7.

### B2. Documented instructions

The following are documented instructions within the meaning of Clause 7.1: this agreement, the Terms and

### B3. Sub-processors

**Notice.** E-Com Shop informs the Customer of any intended addition or replacement by email to the
account's address, at least 30 days before the change. It provides the current list on simple request to
contact@noyaru.com.

**Objection.** During this period, the Customer may object to the change, in writing and giving reasons, to
contact@noyaru.com. The parties consider the objection in good faith. E-Com Shop may propose a solution:
abandoning the change, not entrusting the Customer's data to the new sub-processor, or another suitable
measure. While the objection is being considered, E-Com Shop does not entrust the Customer's data to the
new sub-processor, where technically possible.

**Unresolved objection.** If no solution is found within 30 days of the objection, the Customer may
terminate, without penalty, the services affected by the change. E-Com Shop then refunds the part of the
price already paid corresponding to the unused period, pro rata.

**Urgent replacement (deviation 2).** Where a replacement cannot wait (failure, cessation of business or
security risk at a sub-processor), E-Com Shop informs the Customer as soon as reasonably possible, stating
the reason for the urgency. The Customer keeps its right to object, which it exercises under the procedure
above from the date of this notice.

**Copies of contracts (Clause 7.7(c)).** Public contracts are listed in Annex IV; E-Com Shop provides the
others on request to contact@noyaru.com.

### B4. Data breaches

E-Com Shop notifies the Customer of any personal data breach concerning the data processed under this
agreement without undue delay after becoming aware of it and, where possible, within 48 hours. The
notification is sent by email to the address of the Customer's account. The Customer reaches E-Com Shop's
