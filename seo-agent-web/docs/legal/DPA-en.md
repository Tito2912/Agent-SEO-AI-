## 2. Subject matter and duration

This agreement governs the processing of personal data that E-Com Shop carries out on the
Customer's behalf when the Customer uses the service. It applies for as long as the Customer uses
the service, and then until the return or deletion of the data provided for in section 15.

It does not apply to the processing for which E-Com Shop is itself the controller (account,
subscription, billing, support, security), described in the Privacy Policy (noyaru.com/en/privacy).

## 3. Nature of the processing operations

Collection by crawling the public pages of the sites indicated by the Customer; reading, with the
Customer's authorisation, its code repository, its WordPress site and its search data (Google
Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools); recording, analysis, transmission to artificial
intelligence models to produce fixes and content; writing changes through pull requests or in
WordPress when the Customer asks for it; storage; return or deletion.

## 4. Purposes

Providing the Customer with the features of the service it uses: technical audit of its sites,
suggesting and applying fixes, writing content, tracking search performance, competitor
analysis, assistant.

## 5. Categories of personal data

Any personal data contained in the items the Customer has processed: content of the pages of its
sites (names, business contact details, published texts), content of its code repository and of
its WordPress site, search queries appearing in its Search Console or Bing data, questions asked
to the assistant. Not all of this data is necessarily personal data.

The repositories, sites, content, data and instructions that the Customer provides or has
processed may contain personal data that neither the Customer nor E-Com Shop anticipated. Such
data is then processed under the same conditions as the other data covered by this agreement.

The service is not designed to process special categories of personal data (Article 9 GDPR); the
Customer undertakes not to entrust any such data to it without prior written agreement. This
undertaking does not remove every risk of accidental processing: if either party finds that such
processing has occurred, it informs the other, and the parties agree on the appropriate measures.

## 6. Categories of data subjects

Persons whose data appears on the Customer's sites, in its repository or in its search data: for
example its employees, authors, customers or contacts, and the internet users whose queries
appear in the search statistics.

## 7. Documented Instructions of the Customer

E-Com Shop processes personal data only on the Customer's Documented Instructions, including with
regard to transfers of personal data to a third country or an international organisation. These
instructions are: this agreement, the Terms and Conditions, and the Customer's actions in the
service (adding a site, connecting a repository or a WordPress site, starting an analysis,
requesting a fix, enabling automatic merging).

Where E-Com Shop is required to carry out processing or a transfer by Union or Member State law to
which it is subject, it informs the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless
that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

E-Com Shop immediately informs the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR
or other Union or Member State data protection provisions.

## 8. Confidentiality

E-Com Shop ensures that the persons authorised to process personal data have committed themselves
to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality. Only the
persons who need access to provide the service, maintain it or provide support are authorised to
access the data.

As at the date of this agreement, only E-Com Shop's managing director accesses the data processed
for the Customer and is bound by confidentiality under this agreement. Before any other person
(employee or contractor) is given access, E-Com Shop has them sign a confidentiality undertaking.

## 9. Security

In accordance with Article 32 GDPR, E-Com Shop implements appropriate Technical and Organisational
Measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, taking into account the state of
the art, the costs of implementation, the nature, scope, context and purposes of the processing,
and the risks to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

The measures applied to date are described in Annex 2. E-Com Shop may change them, provided that
the overall level of security is not reduced. This agreement does not refer to any certification
or independent security audit.

## 10. Subprocessors

**General authorisation.** The Customer gives E-Com Shop a general written authorisation to engage
Subprocessors. The list of authorised Subprocessors is set out in Annex 3; E-Com Shop keeps it up
to date and provides it on request at contact@noyaru.com. E-Com Shop's providers are also listed
in its Privacy Policy (noyaru.com/en/privacy).

**Prior notice.** E-Com Shop informs the Customer of any intended addition or replacement of a
Subprocessor, by email to the account address, at least 30 days before the change.

**Objection.** During this period, the Customer may object to the change, in writing and giving
reasons, at contact@noyaru.com. The parties consider the objection in good
faith. E-Com Shop may propose a solution: abandoning the change, not entrusting the Customer's data
to the new Subprocessor, or another appropriate measure. While the objection is being considered,
E-Com Shop does not entrust the Customer's data to the new Subprocessor, where this is technically
possible.

**Unresolved objection.** If no solution is found within 30 days of the objection, the Customer may
terminate, without penalty, the services affected by the change. E-Com Shop then refunds the
portion of the price already paid that corresponds to the unused period, on a pro rata basis.

**Urgent replacement.** Where a replacement cannot wait (failure, cessation of business or a
security risk at a Subprocessor), E-Com Shop informs the Customer as soon as reasonably possible,
stating the reason for the urgency. The Customer retains its right to object, which it exercises
under the procedure above from the date of that notice.

**Obligations of Subprocessors.** E-Com Shop imposes on each Subprocessor, by contract, the same
data protection obligations as those set out in this agreement, in particular sufficient
guarantees to implement appropriate Technical and Organisational Measures. **TO BE COMPLETED**:
the data processing agreement in force with each Subprocessor in Annex 3.

**Liability.** Where a Subprocessor fails to fulfil its data protection obligations, E-Com Shop
remains fully liable to the Customer for the performance of that Subprocessor's obligations
(Article 28(4) GDPR).

## 12. Assistance with data subject requests

Taking into account the nature of the processing, E-Com Shop assists the Customer by appropriate
technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, in responding to requests from
data subjects exercising their rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and
portability, depending on the processing and the applicable obligations. The Customer can itself
delete its projects and their results from the service.

Where E-Com Shop directly receives a request concerning processing carried out on the Customer's
behalf, it forwards the request to the Customer without undue delay, where the request makes it
possible to identify the Customer concerned and forwarding it is appropriate. It does not respond
to the request itself, unless instructed by the Customer.

Requests concerning the processing for which E-Com Shop is itself the controller (account,
subscription, billing, support, security) are governed by the Privacy Policy; they are not
forwarded to the Customer.

## 13. Personal Data Breaches

E-Com Shop notifies the Customer of any Personal Data Breach affecting the data processed under
this agreement without undue delay after becoming aware of it and, where possible, within 48
hours, so that the Customer has time to meet its own deadline for notifying the supervisory
authority.

The notification contains the information available at that time, in particular:

- the nature of the breach;
- the categories of data and of data subjects concerned, and their approximate number, where
  known;
- the likely consequences of the breach;
- the measures taken or proposed to address it and mitigate its effects;
- the name and contact details of a follow-up contact.

Information that is not available immediately is provided afterwards, as soon as it becomes
available, without further undue delay.

The notification is sent by email to the Customer's account address. The Customer can reach
E-Com Shop's follow-up contact at contact@noyaru.com.

## 14. Assistance to the Customer

Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to it, E-Com Shop
provides the Customer with appropriate assistance in ensuring compliance with the obligations
under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR: security of processing (Article 32), notification of a Personal Data
Breach to the supervisory authority and communication to data subjects (Articles 33 and 34), data
protection impact assessments (Article 35) and prior consultation of the supervisory authority
(Article 36).

## 15. Return and Deletion of Personal Data

At the end of the provision of services relating to processing, E-Com Shop, at the Customer's
choice, returns or deletes all personal data processed on its behalf, and deletes existing copies,
unless Union or Member State law requires their storage. The Customer states its choice in
writing at contact@noyaru.com; deleting its projects or its account itself counts as choosing
deletion. Where no choice is stated within 30 days of the end of the services, the data is
deleted.

- **Live database**: when a project or the account is deleted, the corresponding data is erased
  from the live database.
- **Reports and exports**: before deletion, the Customer can download the reports and exports
  offered by the service. These exports contain the analysis results; they do not necessarily
  cover all the personal data processed for the Customer. If the Customer chooses Return of
  Personal Data, E-Com Shop provides it, within 30 days of the request, with the personal data
  processed on its behalf that the exports do not cover, in a structured and commonly used format.
  **TO BE IMPLEMENTED**: the procedure for this return does not exist yet.
- **Backups**: data erased from the live database remains in the backups until they are deleted,
  30 days after their creation. During this residual retention, backups are used only to restore
  the service. **TO BE CONFIRMED**: that the daily backup and its automatic deletion after 30 days
  work in production as configured. **TO BE COMPLETED**: protections applied to the backups
  (storage encryption, restricted access).
- **Copies held by Subprocessors**: E-Com Shop requires Subprocessors to return or delete the
  data at the end of their services (section 10). **TO BE COMPLETED**: the retention period applied
  by each Subprocessor in Annex 3.
- **Changes written to the Customer's systems**: changes already merged into the Customer's
  repository or applied to its WordPress site remain in those systems, under the Customer's
  control. The Customer can revoke the access it granted from those services.

## 16. Audits and compliance information

E-Com Shop makes available to the Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance
with the obligations laid down in Article 28 GDPR and in this agreement. It allows for and
contributes to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or by an auditor mandated
by the Customer.

- **Request**: the Customer sends a written request stating the subject, the scope, the requested
  date and the proposed auditor.
- **Documentation**: E-Com Shop first responds with the available documentation. A document-based
  or on-site audit is organised where that documentation is not sufficient to demonstrate
  compliance with this agreement.
- **Notice**: 30 days for an ordinary audit.
- **Frequency**: as a rule, no more than once in any 12-month period.
- **Confidentiality**: the auditor is bound by professional secrecy or a confidentiality
  undertaking. Information disclosed during the audit remains confidential.
- **Other customers**: the audit gives no access to the data or information of E-Com Shop's other
  customers. Access to systems is limited to what the purpose of the audit requires and must not
  compromise their security.
- **Costs**: each party bears its own audit costs. For an on-site audit, the Customer also
  reimburses the reasonable and documented costs incurred by E-Com Shop, unless the audit reveals a
  material breach of this agreement by E-Com Shop or is required by a supervisory authority.

The notice period and frequency do not apply in the event of a Personal Data Breach, a serious
incident, a request from a supervisory authority or another specific justification. These terms do
not prevent an audit required by applicable law.

## 17. Respective obligations and liability

- **The Customer**: has a legal basis for the processing it entrusts, informs the data subjects,
  only has sites, repositories and data analysed that it is authorised to process, gives
  instructions that comply with the GDPR and checks what is published on its sites.
- **E-Com Shop**: follows the Customer's instructions, Article 28 GDPR and this agreement.

**How the documents fit together.** This agreement supplements the Terms and Conditions. In the
event of a conflict regarding the protection of personal data, this agreement prevails. Neither
the Terms and Conditions nor this agreement limit the obligations that the GDPR imposes directly on
each party.

**Data subjects.** Each party is liable to data subjects under the conditions of Article 82 GDPR.
This agreement does not restrict their rights.

**Liability between the parties.** Each party's liability under this agreement is limited to
direct and foreseeable damage, up to the greater of the amounts paid by the Customer in the 12
months preceding the event giving rise to liability or €1,000, as provided for business customers
in section 10 of the Terms and Conditions. These limits do not apply in the event of gross negligence or wilful misconduct,
nor to liability that a mandatory rule prohibits limiting. They do not reduce the rights of data
subjects under Article 82 GDPR, and an administrative fine remains the responsibility of the party
on which it is imposed.
