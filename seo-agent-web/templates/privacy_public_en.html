Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated.privacy }}

In short: {{ app_name }} processes the data needed to analyze your site, fix your code and bill you. The application is hosted in the United States, and several service providers — including the artificial intelligence providers — are established there. Your data is neither sold nor used to target you with advertising. You can delete your account at any time from My account.

1. Who processes your data

{{ publisher_identity }}, which publishes {{ app_name }}. For any question about your data: {{ support_email }}.

  • Data Controller — {{ publisher.name }} decides how the data relating to your account, your subscription, billing, support and the security of the service is used. This policy describes that processing.
  • Data Processor — when a business customer has us analyze or fix its site, its repository or its search data, and these contain personal data, {{ publisher.name }} may process them on that customer's behalf and on its instructions. Not all the data of the analyzed sites is personal data. For this processing, the customer remains the data controller; a data processing agreement (Article 28 GDPR) sets out its terms and can be requested at {{ support_email }}.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • Providing the service — analyses, fixes, writing, quotas: performance of the contract between us.
  • Billing — performance of the contract, and accounting and tax obligations.
  • Writing to you — address verification, forgotten password, approval of a written page: performance of the contract.
  • Securing the service — preventing abuse, protecting accounts, troubleshooting errors: our legitimate interest in running a secure service.
  • Improving the customer journey — statistics on the first steps of the service: our legitimate interest in identifying usability difficulties. The results presented are aggregated counts.
  • Measuring the campaigns that make Noyaru known — only with your optional consent, requested through an unticked box at sign-up. We link the three labels of the campaign link to your first steps in the service. No tracking cookie, advertising pixel or click identifier is used for this measurement. This data is not sent to any advertising platform. You can withdraw your consent and erase this origin at any time in My account → Privacy. Your account and the features of the service remain available if you refuse or withdraw your consent.

5. International Data Transfers

Several service providers are established outside the European Union, in particular in the United States, including our host. Depending on the provider, these transfers rely on one of the mechanisms provided for by the GDPR:

  • an adequacy decision of the European Commission, including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, for companies certified under that framework;
  • otherwise, the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission, supplemented, where necessary, by additional measures.

You can obtain a description of the safeguards that apply to a given provider by writing to us at {{ support_email }}.

6. What is sent to artificial intelligence models

We send the model the data needed for the feature you use: to write a fix or a page, the public content of the pages concerned and the necessary excerpts of files from your repository; for the assistant, your question and the data of your project relating to it. Under their API terms applicable on the date this policy was last updated, Anthropic and OpenAI do not, by default, use data sent through their APIs to train their models{% if gemini_actif %}; for Google Gemini, this exclusion depends on the plan used{% endif %}. These terms are set by each provider and may change. Do not write personal data in your questions if it is not useful.

7. Data Retention

  • Account, projects and content — as long as your account exists.
  • Sign-up origin — until you withdraw your consent or delete your account. Withdrawal erases the link to the campaign and the date of consent in the live database; the statistics are recalculated without this attribution. Earlier backups disappear at the end of their 30-day retention period.
  • On account deletion — the Stripe subscription is cancelled and the Stripe customer deleted, Search Console access is revoked, the account and its projects are erased; in the audit log, your identity is removed from the events. Backups that still contain them disappear at the end of their 30-day retention period. Only the data that a legal obligation requires us to keep remains, such as invoices.
  • Invoices — for the duration of accounting obligations (ten years in France), at Stripe.
  • Technical logs — the retention period applied by our host, Render, under the plan used.

9. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored as hashes, access tokens and keys encrypted at rest. Where changes are made through pull requests, they require customer approval by default. Certain features may allow automatic merging after the customer has voluntarily enabled that functionality.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. You delete your account yourself, in My account (“Delete my account”); for anything else, write to {{ support_email }}. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority, or, where provided by applicable law, another competent supervisory authority, including in the EU Member State of your habitual residence or place of work.
